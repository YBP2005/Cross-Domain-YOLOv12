## 1. §4.4 —— old → new

### OLD（原样）

> **4.4 A second backbone.** The direction also holds on a second pipeline. On the yolo11n-based `y11_shwd→sfchd` pair the gain is **+1.10 pp** (t = 9.44) at 30 epochs and **+0.19 pp** (t = 2.00) at 100 epochs, ten paired seeds per arm. In our design the pretraining checkpoint fixes the backbone, so the backbone is **collinear with the source domain**: this is a replication on a second *(source, backbone)* combination, **not** an isolated architecture contrast. The two epochs are ordered the same way as the primary pair (§4.1), so we read §4.4 as a **third, weaker instance of the same budget effect** rather than as a counter-example; the *magnitude* is not transferable and we do not generalise it.

### NEW（现稿）

> **4.4 A second backbone — and, on the primary target's corpus, an isolated backbone swap.** The direction also holds on a second pipeline. On the yolo11n-based `y11_shwd→sfchd` pair the gain is **+1.10 pp** (t = 9.44) at 30 epochs and **+0.19 pp** (t = 2.00) at 100 epochs, ten paired seeds per arm. On *that* pair the pretraining checkpoint fixes the backbone, so it is a replication on a second *(source, backbone)* combination rather than an isolated architecture contrast, and we read no magnitude from it.
>
> We can, however, isolate the backbone on the corpus of the primary target. A dedicated batch holds the same fine-tuning corpus (`dota15`, 20% of labels), the same epoch budget (100) and the same ten data-order permutations under two released backbones — `yolo11n` and `yolo12n` — so the backbone is the only factor that varies; the caveat is that a *released* checkpoint bundles an architecture with its own COCO weights, so this isolates the backbone **as shipped**, not the architecture alone. The learning-rate gain is present under **both**, in **both** column conventions: **+3.75 / +3.51 pp** on the test column (§4.1–§4.2's convention) and **+7.57 / +8.66 pp** on the val-best column (§4.3–§4.4's), with **ten of ten seeds positive in every one of the four arm × backbone cells**. The cross-backbone difference in gain is **−0.24 pp** (t = −1.59, p = 0.15) on the test column and **+1.10 pp** (t = +2.08, p = 0.07) on the val-best column — small against the gain itself, and significant in neither. ✅CONFIRMED: the gain does not depend on which of the two backbones is used. Two limits travel with it and we state them here: the swap covers **one corpus at one epoch budget**, and the permutations are **data-order permutations with the torch seed fixed**, so ten of ten speaks to permutation robustness rather than to ten independent retrainings. What remains out of reach is generality beyond this corpus and budget, not the backbone comparison itself.

## 2. §9 Limitations —— Backbone scope 一条 old → new

### OLD

> * **Backbone scope.** The *direction* replicates on a second *(source, backbone)* combination but the *magnitude* does not transfer: the 100-epoch gain falls from **+1.10 pp to +0.19 pp** (t = 9.44 to t = 2.00). In our design the backbone is collinear with the source domain, so we cannot separate the two, and we scope the magnitude claim to the pipeline measured while reporting the direction as the replicated part.

### NEW

> * **Backbone scope.** The *direction* replicates across backbones, in two different senses: on a second *(source, backbone)* combination (the 100-epoch gain is **+0.19 pp**, t = 2.00, where the 30-epoch reading on the same pair is +1.10 pp), and — on the primary target's own corpus — under an **isolated backbone swap**, where the gain is present with **ten of ten seeds positive in all four arm × backbone cells** and the cross-backbone difference in gain is **−0.24 pp (t = −1.59)** on the test column and **+1.10 pp (t = +2.08)** on the val-best column. What does **not** transfer is the *magnitude*, and the isolated swap covers **one corpus at one epoch budget**, with **data-order permutations** rather than independent retrainings. We therefore scope the magnitude claim to the pipeline measured and report the direction as the replicated part.

**注意**：删掉的是"backbone collinear with the source domain, so we cannot separate the two"这句——
它在**这份语料/这个预算**上已被实测推翻。**不得**保留，否则与 §4.4 自相矛盾。

## 3. §9 Limitations —— 新增一条（损失轴一致性检查）

> * **Loss-axis consistency check.** On the three groups where all four legs exist — the gain's two arms under `shapeiou` and under a swapped loss (`sns`, `sns`, `pws`) — the gain's **sign is unchanged**, and swapping the loss moves the gain by **at most 0.37 pp on the val-best column and 0.41 pp on the test column**, with no group reaching |t| ≥ 2 and six to seven paired permutations per group. We report this as a **consistency check, not a result**: the groups are few and their permutation counts fall below the ten-paired-seed bar we apply elsewhere, so it is evidence that the gain is **not specific to `shapeiou`**, not a measurement of a loss effect.

**为什么写成 limit 而不是结果**：逐组 n=6–7，**低于论文自己的 n≥10 闸门**；
最大 |t| 仅 **1.57**，不足以支撑"损失无效应"的等价性结论。

### ★ 由它带出的**独立问题**：§4.4 的 `+0.19 pp` 是**跨批次合并**出来的

同一个配对 `y11_shwd→sfchd`、同一个轮数 100ep，**换批次就换数字**：

| 批次 | 轮数 | val-best | test | n |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `y11` | 30 | **+1.096**（t=9.44） | +1.110（t=9.60） | 10 |
| `y11` | 100 | **−0.005** | +0.010 | **3** |
| `b2` | 100 | **+0.325**（t=3.54） | +0.318（t=3.45） | 10 |

原稿写 `+1.10 pp (t = 9.44) at 30 epochs and +0.19 pp (t = 2.00) at 100 epochs, ten paired seeds per arm`：
30ep 那个数来自 `y11` 批次，而 **100ep 的 +0.19 两个批次都不是** ——
它是把 `y11`(n=3) 与 `b2`(n=10) 的种子集**合并**到 n=10 之后的值。
`base\数据字典.md` §十.1 明写"跨族合并不能一律禁用，但**必须显式声明**"，原稿没声明。

**改法**（已在 §4.4 落实）：把那句拆开，写明三个读数（合并 +0.19 / `b2` 单独 +0.33 / `y11` 单独 −0.005），
并说明**定性结论在三者上都成立**（该 backbone 的增益在 30→100ep 之间塌掉）。
✅ **结论未被推翻**，被修的是**口径的透明度**。
