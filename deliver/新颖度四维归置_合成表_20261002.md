### 4.00 ★★ 对抗组的结论：**审稿人会引 Zhang 2026 拒稿**

> **"No plugin performs consistently across budgets, and several variants switch from positive gains at 1% to
> neutral or negative effects at larger budgets."**（arXiv:2606.30795 §4.3）
> **"The average plugin difference from RTSM decreases from +0.097 AP50 at 1% to −0.856 and −0.816 at 5% and
> 10%, respectively."**
> **"As the labeled subset grows, direct supervision captures a larger fraction of the available target-domain
> signal, leaving less room for noisier and less stable interventions."**

**为什么是这篇**：同**对象**（域自适应目标检测）＋同**机制**（剩余余量决定增益符号）＋同**层**（预算扫描），
三项同时命中。Bouthillier 只到方法学层、Mosbach/Bjorck 在 NLP/LLM 且非检测成对增益 ⇒ **都不能单独作为拒稿依据；Zhang 可以。**

**本文现有分界：部分站得住，但**目前写法**站不住。**

| 站得住 | 站不住 |
