## 1. `§4.4`（原文 L85）

**old（逐字）**

> **4.4 Architecture.** On a second architecture the 30-epoch gain replicates (**+1.27 pp**, t = 6.97) while the 100-epoch gain does not (**−0.01 pp**, t = −0.10) ✅CONFIRMED. The claim is therefore scoped to baselines that have **not converged within the budget**, and we do not generalise it across architectures.

**new**

> **4.4 Architecture.** On a second architecture the *same direction* holds and the magnitude again shrinks with the budget: **+1.10 pp** (t = 9.44) at 30 epochs and **+0.19 pp** (t = 2.00) at 100 epochs, ten paired seeds per arm. The two epochs are ordered the same way as the primary pair (§4.1), so we read §4.4 as a **third, weaker instance of the same budget effect** rather than as a counter-example; the *magnitude* is architecture-dependent and we do not generalise it.

**两条留档说明**（写进脚注或补充材料，不要塞进正文）：

* **口径**：本小节用 **val 侧最优轮 `best_map50_95`**，与 §4.1/§4.2/§5 用的 **test 侧 `test_map50_95`** 不同（见 §3 的披露句）。同一格换列即换数：`y11_shwd→sfchd` 100ep 在 test 侧是 **+0.1930（t=2.036）**、val-best 侧是 **+0.1922（t=2.000）**，**两列结论一致**，所以本节的结论不依赖选列。

## 3. `§9 Limitations`（原文 L146）

**old（逐字）**

> * **Architecture scope.** The gain does not transfer across architectures on the same target at 100 epochs; the claim is scoped to baselines that have not converged within the budget.

**new**

> * **Architecture scope.** The *direction* replicates on the second architecture but the *magnitude* does not transfer: the 100-epoch gain falls from **+1.10 pp to +0.19 pp** (t = 9.44 to t = 2.00). We therefore scope the magnitude claim to the architecture measured and report the direction as the replicated part.

## 4. `§3 Design` —— **新增一句"用哪一列"的披露**（本件唯一无占位、可直接落地的一处）

加在 §3 讲 "Gains are percentage points of mAP50-95, with mAP50 reported alongside." 之后：

> Column convention: the intervention results (§4.1, §4.2, §5) are reported on the **held-out test split** (the corpus's own `test:` entry), whereas the two control subsections (§4.3, §4.4) are reported on the **validation-selected best checkpoint's validation reading**.
